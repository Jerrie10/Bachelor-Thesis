\chapter{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}
\section{Naar Discussion}
As the regions itself are different, few direct comparisons in accessibility score can be made. Comparisons in change of score are however usefull.

Note that the change in mean accessibility is relatively small for both cases, and the increase in minimum accessibility is much larger than the decrease in maximum accessibility for the PC4 case. In the Voronoi case, the maximum accessibility changes very little.
